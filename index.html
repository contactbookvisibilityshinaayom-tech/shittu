A PERSONAL APPROACH TO BOOK VISIBILITY

Meet Shittu.

Welcome to SHITTU BOOK VISIBILITY, a space built around a simple belief: every book has an audience worth finding.

I believe that meaningful book visibility starts with taking the time to understand what an author has created. Before making recommendations, I look at the book itself, the readers it may connect with, and the opportunities that could help it become easier to discover.

My approach is thoughtful, research-led, and centered on helping authors make their work more visible without losing what makes their stories unique.
